## Protein Hydropathy Plot

Calculate sliding-window Kyte–Doolittle scores and plot protein hydropathy.

Each point is the mean hydropathy over a complete window centered on the residue. High regions can suggest hydrophobic segments, and window size controls smoothing. Both transmembrane segments and signal peptides can create peaks.
