## Lipinski Rule of Five

Compute molecular weight, LogP, hydrogen-bond counts, and Lipinski rule violations.

Screen small molecules against common physicochemical properties of orally administered drugs using molecular weight, LogP, and hydrogen-bond donor and acceptor counts. The rule is empirical and helps compare compound properties.
