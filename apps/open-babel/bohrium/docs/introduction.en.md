## Open Babel Molecular Converter

Convert molecule files with coordinate, hydrogen, pH, charge, and force-field options.

Use this tool to exchange small-molecule and structure files between cheminformatics programs. Keep existing coordinates or generate a 2D depiction or a 3D conformer. Output formats preserve different information, so check stereochemistry, bond orders, and charges after conversion.

[Open-source implementation](https://github.com/ZhuYanTech/bohrium-apps/tree/main/apps/open-babel) · [Upstream repository](https://github.com/openbabel/openbabel)
