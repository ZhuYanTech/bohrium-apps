## PDB to FASTA Converter

Extract chain sequences from PDB coordinates or SEQRES records into FASTA.

Observed coordinates and the deposited chain sequence can differ. Coordinate mode extracts visible residues, while SEQRES mode reads the chain sequence listed in the structure file. The converter handles protein and nucleic-acid chains and writes FASTA for downstream analysis.

[Open-source implementation](https://github.com/ZhuYanTech/bohrium-apps/tree/main/apps/pdb-to-fasta) · [Upstream repository](https://github.com/biopython/biopython)
