## Protein aliphatic index

The aliphatic index measures the relative contribution of four aliphatic residues. The Ikai equation is **100 × [A + 2.9V + 3.9(I + L)] / N**. The letters denote residue counts, N includes every standard residue, and the index ranges from 0 to 390.

Mature human insulin chain A (21 residues) scores approximately 88.10, and chain B (30 residues) approximately 84.33. Use the index to compare sequence composition. Assess thermal stability alongside structural information, conditions, and experimental data.
