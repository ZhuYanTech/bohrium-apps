## Protein Structure RMSD Calculator

Measure atom RMSD by structure or chain with rigid fitting, residue ranges, and mass weighting.

Match atoms by chain, author residue number, insertion code, and atom identity before calculating root-mean-square deviation (RMSD). One set of atoms can determine the fit while a different set measures displacement. Compare coverage, model, and fitting settings alongside the RMSD.
