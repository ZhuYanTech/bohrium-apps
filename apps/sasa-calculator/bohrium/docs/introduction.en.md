## Solvent Accessible Surface Area

Calculate residue, chain, and structure solvent accessible surface areas with Shrake–Rupley.

Estimate the solvent-accessible surface area (SASA) of proteins and nucleic acids from probe contact with heavy atoms. Areas sum by chain and structure. For eligible protein residues, normalization to a reference maximum gives relative solvent accessibility (RSA).
