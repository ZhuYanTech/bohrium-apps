## Peptide Digest Mass Calculator

Calculate peptide positions, masses, and ion m/z from in-silico digestion.

Split the input protein sequence according to a selected cleavage rule and report peptide coordinates, missed cleavages, and mass or m/z. Use it to check theoretical peaks and sequence coordinates. Values depend on mass type, ion, and modification settings.

[Open-source implementation](https://github.com/ZhuYanTech/bohrium-apps/tree/main/apps/peptide-mass)
