## Three-to-One Amino Acid Converter

Convert three-letter amino acid codes to one-letter codes with FASTA and unknown-residue options.

Map common three-letter amino-acid codes to one-letter symbols. The converter handles concatenated triplets, common delimiters, and FASTA headers. Keeping unknown codes in brackets makes them easier to review.

[Open-source implementation](https://github.com/ZhuYanTech/bohrium-apps/tree/main/apps/three-to-one)
