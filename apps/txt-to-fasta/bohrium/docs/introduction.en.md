## Text to FASTA Converter

Clean plain-text sequence records and export FASTA.

Convert text containing numbering, wrapped lines, and names into one or more FASTA records. Select validation for DNA, RNA, nucleic acids, or proteins. Keep existing record names or number them in input order.

[Open-source implementation](https://github.com/ZhuYanTech/bohrium-apps/tree/main/apps/txt-to-fasta)
